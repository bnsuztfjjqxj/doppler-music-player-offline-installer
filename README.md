# Doppler Music Player — Free Offline Installer

![Category](https://img.shields.io/badge/Music_Player-blue?style=flat-square)

## About

Doppler is a premium macOS and iOS music player for listening to locally stored music files. Focused on the album listening experience, Doppler features beautiful album art display, gapless playback, ReplayGain normalization, and seamless iPhone sync without iTunes. Doppler's design philosophy — music first, simplicity always — appeals to audiophiles who maintain local music libraries rather than streaming.

## Common Problems & Limitations

- **Apple Only** — macOS and iOS only; no Windows or Android
- **Local Files Only** — No streaming service integration
- **Commercial License** — Paid application
- **Library Size** — Very large local libraries may have performance considerations
